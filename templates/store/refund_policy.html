Return Eligibility

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 3 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase.

How to Request a Return

Contact us via email at {{ site.email|default:"our support email" }} or WhatsApp with your order number and photos of the item. Our team will guide you through the return process.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval status. Approved refunds will be processed via bank transfer or the original payment method within 7-10 business days.

Non-Returnable Items

Items damaged due to misuse, normal wear and tear, or without original packaging are not eligible for return.

Exchanges

If you'd like to exchange an item for a different size, color, or product, please contact us and we will assist you based on stock availability.
